## Findings, ranked by what they cost a reader

### 1. `:active` was missing everywhere (FIXED)
§7 names five composite states. Two `:active` rules existed in the whole tree
and one only changed a cursor, so every button, chip, tab and card was
"unfinished" by §7's own sentence. One rule in `shared.css` fixed all of them.
**Not visually confirmed**: `:active` cannot be forced from script. A human
still has to press something.

### 2. Three of the book's own checks could not detect their gap
- **G13** (`rg -c "shared\.(css|js)"` = 2) matched a COMMENT saying the links
  were absent. It passed on unconverted code and failed once the work was
  right. Pinned to the tag form.
- **G4** (`0 0 0 [0-9]px` = 1) counted every ring-shaped shadow, sweeping in
  the two animations the book says must stay. It read 9 where it claimed 1.
- **G12** (`\.split\b`) matches every JavaScript `.split()` call, reporting 12
  hits in `board.html`, none of them CSS.
A check that cannot fail is the same defect as a rule nobody follows, and this
book shipped three. The §17 pass should re-read §10's remaining checks with
that in mind rather than trusting them.

### 3. A navbar zone could reach zero width
`.nzid` was `flex:none`, so a board whose path is 842px wide pushed identity to
985px and squeezed the find zone — search box included — to **zero**. Only long
path boards show it. Fixed; worth a rule that no zone may reach zero.

### 4. G12 still has no caller
The split button was written for the board-settings dropdown and removed unused
within the hour: the owner asked for a *dropdown*, which is a menu button, and
a split is label-plus-menu. The Offer menu may be the real caller. §10's G12
row should say "when a caller arrives", not "define once".
