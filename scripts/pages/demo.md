# Page kit demo

One page that uses every part of the kit. It is the fixture that `check_page.py` is run against, and the page to open when deciding whether a change to the kit looks right.

## A table with an id column

The id column stays narrow. The two text columns share the rest by how much each holds.

| Id | Decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Which two customers supply the first traced rows | The model is tested on their data first |
| D2 | Who traces the rows | Half a day of the right person settles the vocabulary |
| D3 | Where the catalog lives | It decides the interface.<br>• Inside the application<br>• Or as a service of its own |

## Two columns of equal weight

| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| What problem does this solve, in one sentence | The problem, in the first section of the thesis |
| What does a customer see, and when | The table of phases, in the product page |

## Lists side by side

**What it worked out**

- Files are kept as they arrived.
- The customer decides which parts exist.

**What does not survive**

- Nothing accumulates between runs.
- A correction is overwritten by the next run.
