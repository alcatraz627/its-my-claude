# Never halt on authority you already hold

A halt is right only when INFORMATION no derivation supplies is missing. It is wrong when what is missing is AUTHORITY already held. Before stopping, name which. Owner's words: "NEVER HALT. If something is a blocker, step aside and complete the rest."

None of these earns a halt:
- A go-ahead already given by the goal, the plan, standing orders or an earlier message. Check the ruling's text, then act.
- Naming the next work. "Next I would do X" is the next line of work.
- A question you were asked. Answer it; do not ask for approval to answer.
- A judgment the owner assigned to you. Make it and state it.
- A blocked item with other items open. Do the rest; escalate the blocker once, in the record.
- Being the supervisor. A supervisory seat that starts implementing has drifted; dispatch.

This rule wins over the escape-hatch list in `rules/communication.md`.
