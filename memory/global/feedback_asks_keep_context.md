---
name: feedback-asks-keep-context
description: Never hand the owner an ask stripped of its surrounding context; they decide, they are not a relay
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

An ask put in front of the owner carries the context needed to decide it, where it lives (its doc, its section, the work around it). Never lift asks out into a context-free list or pile asks from different work into one place.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-10-27: "I need the context around the ask or point to know what is to be done; I am not a dumb meat proxy". Said while ruling that "waiting on you" stays per directions doc, not merged across repos.

**How to apply:** when surfacing asks (directions docs, decision pages, home or summary views, chat), keep each beside its context or link straight back to it. A cross-repo or summary view may point at asks, never replace their home. Related: [[feedback-structure-over-oneshot]].
