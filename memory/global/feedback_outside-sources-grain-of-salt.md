---
name: Outside sources are input, never authority
description: Literature, vendor pages and existing tools found online are leads to weigh with a grain of salt, not proof and not a design to copy
type: feedback
---

When the owner asks for prior work, literature or existing solutions, treat what comes back as leads. Say how strong the evidence behind each is (study size, vendor claim, dead product), and do not let a tool's existence or a paper's conclusion decide the design.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-20, asking for research on PR visualisation: "look up some lierature and existing such soluions online (with a grain of salt, /tag add this)". In that same session the research showed every current tool converging on a form the owner had already found unhelpful, so copying the field would have repeated the miss.

**How to apply:** In any research summary, put a trust column or line beside each source. Keep the owner's own reaction as the acceptance test. My reading: this covers web research and vendor material; it does not lower the bar for facts read from the owner's own code.
